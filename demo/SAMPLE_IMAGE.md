# Demo sample photograph

`src/main/assets/earth-apollo17.jpg` is the unchanged NASA photograph **The Blue Marble**, taken by the Apollo 17 crew on December 7, 1972. It is bundled so the native demo works without network access or gallery setup.

- Credit: NASA / Apollo 17 crew.
